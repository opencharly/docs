# 2026.216.2350 — every abbreviation resolves

`IR`, `CUE`, `OCI`, `MCP`, `RPC`, `CalVer`, `ADE`, `RDD`, `SDD`, `SDK` and the probe protocols
(`CDP`, `VNC`, `ADB`) were used across the site with no consistent point of expansion. An
abbreviation nobody expands is noise to a reader meeting it cold, and when it sits in the sentence
carrying a page's main claim, that claim becomes unreadable — which is not meaningfully different
from never having made it.

[The abbreviations](/concepts/00-vocabulary/#the-abbreviations) is now a table on the glossary page,
expanding each one and saying what it means *here* rather than in general.

Two earn the detour. **IR** is the compiler term for the neutral form in the middle — the shared
install plan produced once from a candy list and consumed by the container, VM, cluster, host and
Android backends alike. That is exactly why `pod:` → `vm:` is a keyword change, so a reader who
skipped the abbreviation skipped the mechanism behind the site's headline claim. **CUE** is a schema
language (*Configure, Unify, Execute*) rather than an initialism anyone expands in speech, and the
table says so instead of pretending otherwise.

First uses elsewhere are expanded inline or linked to that table. The home page gets its share
through the generator rather than by hand — the superproject's `README.md` expands CLI, OCI, MCP,
API and CalVer on first use, and `index.md` is regenerated from it.
