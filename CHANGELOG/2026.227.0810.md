# 2026.227.0810 — feat(brand): rebrand to the Agent-in-the-Loop Factory

Moves the site onto OpenCharly's new positioning line — **the fully equipped factory floor for you and your agents** — under the brand name **The Agent-in-the-Loop Factory**.

## What changed

- `astro.config.mjs` — the site `<meta name="description">`. This is the **one remaining hand-maintained copy** of the tagline anywhere: it lives in this repo while the generator that derives every other copy lives in the superproject, so no projection can reach it. Known manual sync point.
- `README.md` — the repo's own tagline copy (it wraps mid-phrase, so a single-line grep for the old wording does not find it).
- `concepts/00-vocabulary.md` — gains the bridge paragraph reconciling the factory brand with the confectionery schema. This is the page where a reader is most likely to feel the seam.
- `concepts/02-one-recipe-many-molds.md` — drops the closing pun "A fully stocked kitchen really does ship with the sink", which only worked as a callback to the retired tagline. The tip and the "kitchen-sink dev boxes" idiom stay.
- Regenerated trees via `task docs:sync`.

## The metaphors reconcile — they were never two

`VISION.md` already read *"a whole factory ready to produce every candy imaginable."* The brand promotes the factory half of the project's own sentence. Factory = the line and the loop; candy = the product; **kitchen** — gourmet, pantry, buffet — is what retires. Confectionery vocabulary stays and is reinforced, because it names the output.

## What deliberately did NOT change

- **No route or filename moved.** Titles, labels and prose only. `/recipes/` and every `/concepts/…` path is linked from the superproject README, from the generator's link-rewrite table, and from cross-references the build hard-fails on.
- **"Recipe cards" and "One recipe, many molds" are kept** — a production line runs on recipe cards and a mold is factory tooling; these read as *more* on-brand after the rebrand.
- **The two pages that blockquote `VISION.md` tenets verbatim** (`concepts/04-you-and-your-agents.md`, `guides/authoring-a-plugin.md`) are untouched, because `VISION.md` is untouched and the quotes remain accurate.

## The home page is now derived, not restated

The hero tagline, meta description and `<title>` are no longer hardcoded in the generator — they are lifted from the first bold line of the superproject `README.md`. The previous generator matched that line by its **literal wording**, so changing the tagline would have silently printed it twice on this page.

## Verification

- `task docs:drift` — regeneration is a no-op across 889 generated pages.
- Production build (`npm run build`): **909 pages, exit 0, zero warnings**; the whole-site link gate passes, proving no title change moved a route.
- Double-render check: the tagline appears **0×** in the page body and once as the hero; the bold-opening bridge paragraph survives intact.
