## 3. The Debian-family `distro:` trap

`recipes/internals/vm-spec.md` gained the omission trap: on a Debian-family cloud image, leaving
`source.distro` unset rendered `openssh` plus `systemctl enable --now sshd` — a unit Debian and
Ubuntu do not have — and with cloud-init's deliberate `mask ssh.socket` that left a
socket-activated sshd masked and never started. The guest booted fully and served nothing.

## Scope: why this is a narrow page set

Only pages whose full regenerated output is revert-free are included. A wholesale regeneration also
wants to delete prose whose candy source is still in flight on a superproject branch: because a
projection merges BEFORE the source that pins it, prose can be correct and landed here while its
source is unmerged, and regenerating from a tree that lacks that source drags the page backwards.
The diff gives no hint, because a reverted projection looks exactly like one that was never stale.
