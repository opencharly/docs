## Summary

Head `d42a49a2434f0dd6a2ab67bf1b75a6f9ee7b2d2e` — 2 files changed, 2 insertions(+), 2 deletions(-).

**The defect.** This repo's recipe page for the `container-nesting` candy is a projection of the corpus: its `Env` row carried `CHARLY_BUILD_ENGINE=podman` — the **retired builder selector**. The contract retired it: `EngineConfig.Build`, `ResolvedRuntime.BuildEngine` and the `CHARLY_BUILD_ENGINE` key are gone from `spec/hostenv`, and a leftover `engine.build: docker` is INERT (`spec/hostenv/retired_build_selector_test.go`). The site therefore taught every reader a knob that cannot be turned.

**The RCA.**

- **Mechanism** — the site is a projection: the deploy workflow regenerates the generated half of the content tree from three pinned inputs — the corpus (`marketplace` gitlink), the charly source commit, and the umbrella commit — and the recipe pages are projected from the corpus. The owner had already cut the name out of the candy, the skill body and the README in one cutover (opencharly/layer-container-nesting#16, tag `v2026.302.0925`), and the corpus republished the owner's corrected body (opencharly/marketplace#601, squash `b53310d`, tag `v2026.302.0951`) — but this repo's `marketplace` gitlink still pointed at the corpus commit from before that republication, so every regeneration republished the old row.
- **Missed control** — the drift gate proves FIDELITY to the pinned inputs: the pinned sources regenerate their own old pages perfectly, so a stale corpus pin is invisible to it. That blind spot is the corpus pin's alone — the workflow already reports the currency of all three pins (the `check_pin_currency.sh` step, "Reported, never failed"), because a pin that is behind is an advance available NOW and the remedy is a regeneration PR, not a gate.
- **Blast radius** — every reader of the site's container-nesting recipe: the page taught a retired selector that the runtime cannot honour.
- **Root fix** — advance the one corpus gitlink to the corpus's tip and regenerate in the same commit. The site may not hand-edit a projection (it is generated output), and the corpus may not edit the site.
